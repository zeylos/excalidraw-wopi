{{- if or (not .Values.config.publicUrl) (not .Values.config.wopiAllowedOrigins) }}

WARNING: config.publicUrl or config.wopiAllowedOrigins is empty.
The service refuses every POST /launch call with an empty allowed-origins list.
A wrong publicUrl breaks launch and discovery both.
Set both values before you register this release with a WOPI host.
{{- end }}
